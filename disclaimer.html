Privacy: newsletter sign-ups

When you subscribe to The Green Brief we receive the email address and any optional name, company and role you give. We use them only to send the brief and to reply to you, and we do not sell them. Sign-ups are saved to GABI's own subscriber list, kept in our company email and document account, and we send you one welcome email when you join. If that route is unavailable, sign-ups are passed to us by a backup email form service (currently FormSubmit), which may keep submissions for a short time to deliver them. To unsubscribe, or to ask us to correct or delete your details, reply "unsubscribe" to any edition or email jeff@gabi.ph. We handle personal data in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.
