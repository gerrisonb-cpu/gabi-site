Services

FPA registration and regulatory services

We help manufacturers, importers and foreign principals register and keep farm inputs on the Philippine market. Most of our work is with the Fertilizer and Pesticide Authority (FPA).

  • Product registration
  • Renewals and amendments
  • Labels and FPA comments
  • EUPs and field trials
  • Support for foreign principals
  • FDA registration
  • Regulatory intelligence
  • Market entry and distributors
01

Product registration

We prepare and file registration applications with the Fertilizer and Pesticide Authority (FPA) for pesticides and fertilizers, and follow them through to an FPA decision.

  • Gap check of your dossier against current FPA requirements
  • Registration documents, specifications and supporting studies, packaged for filing
  • Coordination of product samples for FPA confirmatory analysis
  • Follow-up and replies to FPA queries until a decision is issued
02

Renewals and amendments

Registrations need upkeep. We track expiry dates and handle the paperwork that keeps a product on the market.

  • Renewal applications for registered products
  • Amendments and reclassification filings when FPA requires them
  • Replies to FPA deficiency and compliance letters
  • FPA licence applications and follow-up
03

Labels and FPA comments

Labels often draw FPA comments. We draft labels to FPA format and revise them when comments come back.

  • Draft labels for new registrations
  • Revisions in response to FPA comments, with a clear change record
  • Label checks before printing and re-submission
04

EUPs and field trials

New products usually need local data. We prepare Experimental Use Permit applications and coordinate the field work FPA asks for.

  • Experimental Use Permit (EUP) dossiers and filing
  • Trial protocols agreed with an FPA-accredited researcher
  • Scheduling and tracking of bioefficacy and residue trials
  • Trial reports taken into the registration dossier
05

Support for foreign principals

Foreign manufacturers need a Philippine party to deal with the FPA. Where agreed, we act as that local registrant and point of contact.

  • Holding registrations in the Philippines on behalf of a foreign principal, under written terms
  • Letters of authorization and third-party authorizations for registrations based on your source
  • A single local contact for FPA correspondence
06

FDA registration

Some products, such as certain household and public-health products, are regulated by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) rather than the FPA. We help you confirm which agency applies and prepare the FDA filing.

  • Advice on whether a product falls under FPA or FDA
  • FDA registration requirements and dossier preparation
  • Review of supporting reports against FDA format
07

Regulatory intelligence

We follow FPA circulars, notices and prices so you hear about changes early.

  • The free Green Brief, with every item checked against a dated public source
  • Updates on FPA policy changes and draft circulars
  • Comments on draft FPA rules during public consultation
08

Market entry and distributors

Registration is one step. We help you decide where a product fits in the Philippine market and who could sell it.

  • Market-entry assessment by crop and segment
  • Identifying and evaluating possible Philippine distributors
  • Market development planning for new product categories
How we work

From first email to registration

  1. Gap checkYou share product details and the study index, under NDA if you prefer. We list what is missing against current FPA requirements.
  2. ProposalA written scope for the work you need: one product or a portfolio.
  3. EUP and trialsWhere FPA needs local data, we file the EUP and coordinate trials with an accredited researcher.
  4. Dossier and filingWe package and file the dossier, coordinate confirmatory samples and answer FPA queries.
  5. Registration and upkeepOnce registered, we handle label changes and renewals.

What we do not promise. Permits and registrations are issued at the FPA's (or FDA's) discretion. We do not guarantee approvals, outcomes or FPA timelines. Our quotes show our professional fee separately from government fees and trial or laboratory costs.

Have a product to register?

Send the product name, active ingredient, formulation and intended crops. We will reply with the FPA path and what it needs.
