1Global industry and trends

EU does not renew buprofezin approval

Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2517, adopted on 12 November 2026 and published in the Official Journal on 13 November, does not renew the approval of buprofezin. Buprofezin is deleted from Part A of the Annex to Implementing Regulation (EU) No 540/2011. Member States must withdraw authorisations for buprofezin products by 3 June 2027, and any grace period under Article 46 of Regulation (EC) No 1107/2009 expires by 3 December 2027. The regulation enters into force on 3 December 2026. Recital 9 identifies buprofezin as meeting the human thyroid endocrine-disruptor criteria. It supersedes reliance on the previously scheduled 15 March 2027 approval expiry.

Why it mattersBuprofezin users and suppliers with EU-linked markets have a fixed timeline to plan around.

Sources: EUR-Lex, Regulation (EU) 2026/2517, 13 Nov 2026 · Global Agriculture, 18 Nov 2026

UK and EU agree a food and farming standards deal; UK names three actives

The UK and EU reached a Sanitary and Phytosanitary agreement on 20 November 2026, with an application date of 1 March 2028. GOV.UK guidance published on 20 November names cinmethylin, isoflucypram and pydiflumetofen as Category 1. They remain on the GB market under existing GB authorisations until the EU decides. If the EU non-approves an active after the application date, sale ceases within three months of that decision and final use and disposal within nine months. If non-approval comes before the application date, Category 2 transition rules apply. Implementing legislation is still to be laid.

Why it mattersSuppliers of these three actives have a rule set to watch, but no date yet that affects them.

Sources: GOV.UK food blog, 20 Nov 2026 · GOV.UK, guidance for manufacturers, 20 Nov 2026 · GOV.UK, guidance for farmers and growers

Bayer Roundup settlement gets final court approval

A Missouri court granted final approval to Bayer's Roundup class settlement on 20 November 2026. Judge Timothy Boyer approved up to US$7.25 billion over two decades, with claimant payments of about US$10,000 to US$165,000. The decision remains appealable.

Why it mattersThe approval settles one large legal exposure on glyphosate, but the appeal route is still open.

Sources: AP, 20 Nov 2026 · Bayer release, 20 Nov 2026

2Company and innovation news

None of these items is tied to the Philippines in the sources cited. They are included as signals of where crop-protection technology and capital are heading.

Bionema reports trial results and seeks distribution partners

Trial data published on 16 November 2026 showed western flower thrips control in UK strawberries rising from 58% to 97.5% using Bionema's Incapsulex with a living biocontrol agent. Bionema says it is seeking field-trial hosts and distribution or licensing partners.

Why it mattersA formulation company openly looking for partners is a market signal, not a confirmed opening in the Philippines.

Source: Global Agriculture, 16 Nov 2026

SICIT opens a €40 million biostimulant plant in Mexico

SICIT opened a €40 million biostimulant ingredients plant at León, Guanajuato, Mexico, on 19 November 2026. It is the company's second plant after Arzignano, Italy. It covers three platforms: collagen-derived peptides from leather-tanning by-products, Patagonian seaweed extracts, and botanical extracts from locally grown Moringa oleifera. No capacity tonnage was disclosed. The stated export targets are the United States, Latin America and China.

Why it mattersThe source makes no statement about the Philippines or ASEAN.

Source: Global Agriculture, 19 Nov 2026

HELM launches Reviton Ultra herbicide

HELM Crop Solutions announced Reviton Ultra on 16 November 2026. It combines tiafenacil (Group 14) and glufosinate (Group 10) for burndown and selected crop uses. The notice does not give a registration number or approval date.

Why it mattersA new two-active herbicide mix; no registration number or approval date was found in the source.

Source: HELM Crop Solutions notice, 16 Nov 2026

Crystal Crop Protection IPO closes about 17 times subscribed

The Crystal Crop Protection IPO ran from 18 to 20 November 2026 at a price band of ₹259 to ₹272 and closed at approximately 17.07 times overall subscription. Listing on NSE and BSE is scheduled for 26 November, after this window.

Why it mattersListing is still to come, so the market's response is not yet known.

Source: Groww

Chinese glyphosate technical is flat at a high level

China's NBS reports glyphosate 95% technical at 29,000.0 yuan per tonne for 1 to 10 November 2026, down 35.7 yuan or 0.12% from 29,035.7 for 21 to 31 October. Earlier prints were 28,250.0 for 21 to 30 September (up 4.27% on the prior print) and 29,035.7 for 11 to 20 October (up 2.78%). The NBS basis is wholesale or circulation prices across about 2,000 wholesale enterprises in 31 provinces, municipalities and autonomous regions, including circulation costs, trading profit and applicable taxes. These are not ex-works factory-gate prices. Global Agriculture reports the latest move as 0.1%.

Why it mattersTechnical-grade glyphosate has stopped rising but has not fallen back.

Sources: NBS, 1 to 10 Nov, 14 Nov 2026 · NBS, 21 to 31 Oct, 04 Nov 2026 · Global Agriculture, 18 Nov 2026

FPA: no circular newer than 27 October

The FPA memorandum circular index checked on 22 November 2026 has no November 2026 circular. Its newest entries are dated 27 October 2026: MC 2026-17, Requirement for Batch Number or Lot Number on all Fertilizer Products Labels and Packaging; MC 2026-16, Authorization of the FPA Regional Field Units to accept Payment of Application Fees for License To Operate (LTO) as Fertilizer Handler (National-Level); and Administrative Order No. 2026-01, Emergency Regulatory Measures for Facilitating Fertilizer Pesticide and Other Agricultural Chemicals Trade and Product Registration During Emergency, Armed Conflict, or Severe Supply Disruption. Signing dates and effectivity were not readable in the files checked. MC 2026-04, 2026-13 and 2026-15 are absent from the index, which does not establish that they were never issued.

Why it mattersThe effectivity and transition terms of MC 2026-17 are not yet readable in the published files.

Sources: FPA memorandum circulars index · FPA AO 2026-01, 27 Oct 2026

Sugarcane RSSI: relief reaches ₱1.44 billion and seed farms spread

DSWD disbursement to RSSI-affected sugarcane farmers and laborers reached ₱1,439,654,725 for 139,833 beneficiaries as of 14 November 2026, made up of 75,174 farmers and 64,659 laborers. Negros Occidental accounts for ₱1,201,912,600 and 116,408 beneficiaries, including 59,072 farmers and 57,336 laborers. Separately, Negros Occidental established RSSI-recovery seed farms for PHILSURIN's PSR 03-171 variety in eight LGUs: Sagay City, Cadiz City, Manapla, Victorias City, E.B. Magalona, Silay City, Talisay City and Murcia. Sagay dispersal was on 11 November and the Cadiz handover on 16 November.

Why it mattersRelief and replanting are both moving; laborers make up 64,659 of the 139,833 people assisted.

Sources: Digicast Negros, DSWD relief, 15 Nov 2026 · Digicast Negros, eight seed farms, 17 Nov 2026
