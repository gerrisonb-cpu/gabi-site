The Green Brief - 8 November 2026

Sunday, 8 November 2026 · Covers 1 to 8 November 2026 · Every news item checked against a dated public source

This week in Philippine & global agrochem

Q3 crop-protection sales contracted across several major companies, with Asia-Pacific also under pressure. PAGASA says a very strong El Niño is present, and DA estimates full-year 2026 palay production could be around 1 million MT below 2025. FPA Week 44 puts DAP at ₱3,346.03 per 50 kg bag, up 13.0% since Week 41, while Chinese glyphosate technical rose 2.8% in the October NBS print.

1Global industry and trends

Q3 crop-protection contraction reaches Asia-Pacific

BASF Agricultural Solutions' Q3 crop-protection sales fell 23.8% to $1.30 billion. Bayer Crop Science crop-protection sales fell 4.0% to $2.39 billion, and the Crop Science division's sales in Asia-Pacific fell 11.8% on an adjusted basis. FMC group revenue fell 80% to $106 million; Asia excluding India fell to $91 million from $99 million.

Why it mattersLower originator prices narrow the price gap that generic products must compete against in the Philippines.

Sources: Global Agriculture, 06 Nov 2026 · FMC results, 29 Oct 2026

EU residue limits took effect on 5 November

Commission Regulation (EU) 2026/2289 sets the prothioconazole maximum residue limit on rice at 0.03 mg/kg. Adopted on 15 October and published on 16 October 2026, it entered into force on 5 November. The regulation also revises limits for acequinocyl, deltamethrin, dodine, maleic hydrazide and pinoxaden.

Why it mattersRice bound for the EU must meet this residue ceiling, making pre-harvest intervals relevant to export plans.

Source: EUR-Lex, Regulation (EU) 2026/2289, 16 October 2026

PMFAI calls for more Indian technical production

At its 4 November Mumbai meeting, PMFAI called for more domestic synthesis of technical-grade pesticides and intermediates amid raw-material and logistics pressures. It also described its presentations to the Rotterdam Convention's Chemical Review Committee on fipronil, profenofos, phenthoate, ethion, quinalphos, cypermethrin EC at 10% and 35%, and emamectin benzoate 5% SG.

Why it mattersChemical Review Committee attention is an early signal of regulatory pressure, ahead of national action.

Source: Global Agriculture, 06 Nov 2026

Also in the news

  • Brazilian pesticide prices are sliding under generic competition. Ato No. 69 of 4 November covers 14 registration requests, not approvals. The 78 registrations under Ato No. 68 date to 28 October and are background. Source: Global Agriculture, 06 Nov 2026
  • Hikal announced a multi-year manufacturing agreement with an unnamed European agrochemical company on 2 November. Commercial supplies are expected towards the end of FY 2026-27; the molecule and contract value were not disclosed. Source: Hikal press release, 02 Nov 2026
  • Biotalys received Dutch permission for large-scale demonstration and commercial field trials of EVOCA NG against Botrytis and powdery mildew on tomatoes, cucumbers and strawberries. This is trial permission, not full product registration. Source: Biotalys release, GlobeNewswire, 03 Nov 2026

Why it mattersGeneric competition, manufacturing agreements and biological trials show different stages of products moving towards market.

2Company and innovation news

None of these announcements names the Philippines as a market. They are included as signals of where crop-protection technology is heading.

Anhui Huaxing and Silicon Gene agree to develop RNA pesticides

Anhui Huaxing Chemical Industry, a Qilu Pharmaceutical Group subsidiary, signed a technology development agreement with Shanghai's Silicon Gene on 28 October. Reported on 2 November, the agreement covers development and field application of RNA-based pesticides in China. No individual molecule is named.

Why it mattersThe programme covers precision pest control, plant immune induction and nutritional enhancement.

Source: AgroPages, 02 Nov 2026

Vicome prepares a biopesticide line for March 2027

Shandong Vicome Greenland Chemical is commissioning a biopesticide line, with commercial launch expected in March 2027. Its rice blast biopesticide has completed public notification. The Bacillus velezensis GSBM05 strain and technology transfer agreement dates to 10 September 2026. Vicome also holds two microbial fertiliser registrations covering grapes, cucumbers, tomatoes and potatoes.

Why it mattersThe company is developing both microbial fertiliser and crop-protection uses.

Source: AgroPages, 02 Nov 2026

Sharda Cropchem reports higher Q2 FY27 revenue and profit

Revenue rose 13% to Rs. 1,052 crore, EBITDA rose 18% to Rs. 164 crore and PAT rose 23% to Rs. 92 crore. Gross margin was 35.9%.

Why it mattersSharda's revenue and profit both grew in the quarter.

Source: AgroSpectrum India, Sharda Q2 FY27, 03 Nov 2026

Dr Heinisch announces nano copper registrations in four Indian states

Dr Heinisch Agro Solutions India announced on 5 November that its AgroCuprum nano copper fertiliser is registered in Himachal Pradesh, Haryana, Karnataka and Rajasthan. The company recommends it for tomato, chilli, capsicum, cucumber and pomegranate.

Why it mattersThe announcement concerns nutrient technology for vegetables and high-value crops.

Source: PTI via The Week, 05 Nov 2026

The NBS average for glyphosate technical, 95%, was 29,035.7 yuan per tonne for 11 to 20 October 2026, up 785.7 yuan or 2.8% from 28,250.0 yuan for 21 to 30 September. Covered on 2 November, this is an October price, not a November quote.

4Emerging crops and pests

Very strong El Niño and a lower 2026 palay estimate

PAGASA said on 4 November that a very strong El Niño is present. For November, it expects below-normal to way-below-normal rainfall over most of the country. DA estimates that full-year 2026 palay production could be around 1 million MT below 2025, and expects more significant El Niño effects in the first half of 2027. The 2026 decline is not attributable solely to El Niño. Recorded agricultural losses stood at ₱8.57 billion as of 21 October, across 288,058 farmers and 291,154 hectares.

Why it mattersThe palay figure is a full-year estimate against 2025, while the peso damage tally is a dated assessment of losses already recorded.

Sources: PAGASA press release, 04 Nov 2026 · DA · Global Agriculture, 05 Nov 2026

PCAFI warns of pest pressure, including RSSI

PCAFI president Danilo Fausto warned that drought could intensify fall armyworm, stemborer and red-striped soft scale insect on Negros sugarcane. This is a warning, not a confirmed new outbreak. No official Regional Crop Protection Center or BPI-CPMD pest advisory dated 1 to 8 November 2026 was located in the routes searched. That is not proof none was issued.

Why it mattersA drought-related pest warning should be kept distinct from an official outbreak advisory.

Source: BusinessMirror, 04 Nov 2026

RSSI candidate insecticides: the dated registration gap

A DOST-PCAARRD summary of 5 June 2025 records the National Crop Protection Center identifying buprofezin, dinotefuran, phenthoate, pymetrozine and thiamethoxam as having potential against RSSI. None was registered for use on sugarcane at that time. Their registration status on 8 November 2026 is not established here.

Why it mattersPotential against a pest does not establish an approved sugarcane use.

Source: DOST-PCAARRD, 05 Jun 2025

Western Visayas smart-greenhouse project prepares its first harvest

A 7 November preview scheduled the VEHCSA project's first harvest in Kalibo, Aklan, for that day. The project has an $8 million Korean grant through KOICA and runs from November 2023 to December 2027 across sites in Aklan, Iloilo, Antique and Negros Occidental. Crops include lettuce, bell pepper and cherry tomato. The Tigbauan, Iloilo site inauguration is scheduled for 20 November.

Why it mattersThis is protected-culture production across four provinces; the preview does not confirm completed harvest volumes.

Source: Daily Guardian, 07 Nov 2026

5Primer: The neonicotinoids, IRAC Group 4A

Reference material, not dated news. This primer is based on the IRAC Mode of Action Classification.

Group 4A: systemic insecticides acting on nerve receptors

IRAC Mode of Action Classification places neonicotinoids in Group 4A, nicotinic acetylcholine receptor competitive modulators. Members include imidacloprid, acetamiprid, thiamethoxam, clothianidin and dinotefuran. They are systemic and move upwards in the plant, with activity against sap-feeding pests such as aphids, whiteflies and planthoppers.

Why it mattersGroup 4A identifies the shared mode of action, even when product names differ.

Changing between two Group 4A actives is not rotation

Cross-resistance within Group 4A is common. Brown planthopper resistance across East and Southeast Asia is associated with enhanced cytochrome P450 detoxification. IRAC guidance calls for alternation with a different mode-of-action group. Thiamethoxam is converted to clothianidin in plants and insects, so those two are not a rotation pair.

Why it mattersSwitching from imidacloprid to thiamethoxam does not change the mode-of-action group.
