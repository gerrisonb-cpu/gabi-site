The Green Brief - 18 October 2026

Sunday, 18 October 2026 · Covers 11 to 18 October 2026 · Every item checked against a dated public source

This week in Philippine & global agrochem

Iloilo declared a province-wide state of calamity over the red-striped soft scale insect (RSSI), and the pest is now confirmed in Bukidnon, Mindanao. USDA cut its Philippine raw sugar forecast, FPA's Week 41 fertilizer prices were mixed, and fertilizer trade through the Strait of Hormuz remains constrained. Abroad: an EU data-protection proposal, a Rotterdam Convention outcome on three organophosphates, and new fungicide and biological launches.

1Global industry and trends

Fertilizer trade through the Strait of Hormuz remains constrained

Shipments through the strait were close to zero in early September, and April 2026 trade data show urea exports from Hormuz-dependent economies down 83% and ammonia down 75% year on year. Sulfur peaked at around USD 1,000 to 1,200 per tonne and was last cited near USD 850 per tonne on 7 October.

Why it mattersThe Philippines imports nearly all of its nitrogen and phosphate, so this is an upstream pressure on local fertilizer prices.

Sources: Global Agriculture, 13 Oct 2026 · Brownfield, 7 Oct 2026

Industry study: 86 of 180 EU active substances could face higher renewal risk

An analysis by Acumen Regulatory Consultants for CropLife Europe says a single EU-wide data-protection clock proposed under the Food and Feed Safety Omnibus could shorten effective protection, with renewal studies costing EUR 5 to 20 million plus EUR 1 to 2 million for product renewals. It is an industry estimate of risk, not a forecast of withdrawals.

Why it mattersEU withdrawals can affect the global availability of active ingredients used in Philippine-registered products; minor crops such as fruit and vegetables are the most exposed.

Source: CropLife Europe, 16 Oct 2026

Rotterdam Convention committee did not recommend listing profenofos, phenthoate or quinalphos

Meeting in Rome on 15 to 18 September 2026, the Chemical Review Committee deferred profenofos and found no notification for phenthoate or quinalphos that met the listing criteria. This is not a safety clearance, and profenofos may return to a future agenda.

Why it mattersAll three are organophosphates in use in the region.

Source: BRS Secretariat presentation, 5 Oct 2026

India extends its minimum import price on glufosinate to 12 April 2027

DGFT Notification No. 43/2026-27 extends the minimum import price condition on glufosinate and its salts by six months on unchanged terms.

Why it mattersIt is an Indian import measure, not an export restriction, so it does not directly limit supply to the Philippines.

Source: DGFT Notification No. 43/2026-27, 12 Oct 2026

First Belarusian potash cargo bound for the US in four years

A 30,000-tonne cargo loaded near St Petersburg was due at the Port of New Orleans in mid-October 2026, after US sanctions on Belarusian producers were rolled back earlier in the year. The volume is small next to annual US imports.

Why it mattersPotash (MOP 0-0-60) is the grade where reopened supply could eventually ease landed costs, but one cargo is not a market shift.

Source: Al Jazeera, 14 Oct 2026

Also in the news

  • Brazil is prioritising implementation of its Bioinputs Law (reported 12 October 2026). Source: Brazil Ministry of Agriculture (MAPA) bioinputs material, 15 Jan 2026
  • Syngenta submitted a label expansion for its HiFlex bicyclopyrone corn herbicides, targeting approval in 2027. Source: Syngenta release via SeedQuest, 12 Oct 2026
  • Environmental groups sued the US EPA over Clean Water Act criteria for atrazine. Source: Center for Biological Diversity, 14 Oct 2026

Why it mattersBackground signals on biologicals regulation and herbicide label pressure abroad.

2Company and innovation news

None of these announcements names the Philippines as a market. They are included as signals of where crop-protection and crop-nutrition technology is heading.

Sumitomo Chemical launches Velera (metyltetraprole) fungicide in Paraguay

Launched on 14 October 2026 for soybean diseases, metyltetraprole sits in FRAC subgroup 11A by the company's account and is positioned against QoI-resistant pathogens. Paraguay is its first commercial launch outside Japan, with reviews ongoing in Brazil, Europe and other countries.

Why it mattersA new resistance-management tool entering global rollout; the Philippines is not named among the pending markets.

Source: Sumitomo Chemical, 14 Oct 2026

Molagri raises USD 5.8 million for virus-like-particle biopesticides

The Toronto-based start-up's engineered virus-like particles make the target pest's own cells produce an insecticidal payload. Its initial focus is tree-nut and tree-fruit pests.

Why it mattersEarly-stage technology: field efficacy, manufacturing cost and regulatory approval are not yet established.

Source: AgFunderNews, 14 Oct 2026

Bayer Crop Science in two biological-nutrition deals

Switch Bioworks announced a collaboration with Bayer Crop Science on programmable biological nitrogen, initially focused on corn. Separately, Bayer will market and distribute INERA's biological crop-nutrition solution in India.

Why it mattersAn R&D collaboration and a distribution deal, not a proven replacement for fertilizer.

Sources: PR Newswire, 13 Oct 2026 · ANI, 16 Oct 2026

BioConsortia raises USD 15 million

The funding came from an entity affiliated with John Pasquesi of Otter Capital. The company names Asia among expansion markets for Always N, its nitrogen-fixing seed treatment, and reports an average of +4.4 bu/ac over three seasons of corn trials for its Zaffre biostimulant (company-reported).

Why it mattersA nitrogen-fixing seed treatment would need FPA classification before it could enter the Philippine market.

Source: BioConsortia release via Business Wire, 9 Oct 2026

Philippines presents USD 57.4 million in agrifood investment proposals

At FAO's Hand-in-Hand Investment Forum in Rome (13 to 16 October 2026), the country presented projects led by coffee (about USD 26.4 million) and seaweed (about USD 24.8 million), plus mango, jackfruit and cocoa. These are proposals seeking financing, not funded investments.

Why it mattersIf financed, the coffee and cacao projects could create new demand for crop inputs.

Sources: Malaya, 13 Oct 2026 · Panay News, 14 Oct 2026

Chinese glyphosate technical price up for a second straight 10-day period

4Emerging crops and pests

Iloilo declares a province-wide state of calamity over the red-striped soft scale insect

The provincial board approved the declaration on 15 October 2026. As of 5 October, 9,491 farmers and 10,342.72 hectares in 27 LGUs were affected, with estimated losses of about ₱1.18 billion (one report prints ₱1.81 billion; a final provincial assessment has not been seen).

Why it mattersInfestation rates reach 99% in Passi City and 98% in Dueñas, and the province plans biocontrol laboratories prioritising Metarhizium.

Sources: PNA, 14 Oct 2026 · PIA, 16 Oct 2026 · Manila Times, 17 Oct 2026

Iloilo quarantine: planting material leaving red zones must be disinfected

Executive Order 143, issued on 16 October, sets up red, orange, yellow and green zones. Moving planting material out of red zones requires disinfection with biological agents or FPA-accredited chemicals, a disinfection certificate, and written clearances from the Provincial Agriculture Office and SRA.

Why it mattersThe order names FPA-accredited products, so the registration status of disinfectants now matters to cane growers moving planting material.

Sources: Panay News, 16 Oct 2026 · Manila Times, 17 Oct 2026

RSSI confirmed in Bukidnon, Mindanao

About 1,500 hectares are infested across Dangcagan, Don Carlos, Kitaotao, Maramag, Quezon and Valencia City, after a first detection in Kitaotao in June 2026. More than 65% of the area has been treated with Beauveria bassiana, Metarhizium anisopliae, lacewings, earwigs, neem oil and surfactants, plus unnamed targeted chemicals.

Why it mattersThe outbreak now reaches a Mindanao milling district ahead of the 2026-27 milling season, which opens in November.

Source: Philippine Daily Inquirer, 17-18 Oct 2026

USDA cuts the Philippine raw sugar forecast to 1.7 million MT

USDA FAS Manila forecasts 2026-27 raw sugar 8% below the revised 1.85 million MT for 2025-26, citing RSSI, El Niño and higher input costs. Citing SRA, it reports RSSI on 33% (139,364 ha) of 421,606 ha planted as of 28 August 2026.

Why it mattersPlanters are spending more on insecticides, sometimes with several applications and different chemical types, which is a resistance-management concern as much as a cost one.

Source: USDA FAS GAIN report RP2026-0032, 8 Oct 2026

PAGASA: strong to very strong El Niño expected to last into 2027

PAGASA's ENSO monitoring says El Niño is present, may peak before the end of 2026 and is expected to persist into the first half of 2027.

Why it mattersUSDA links drier, warmer conditions to faster RSSI reproduction.

Sources: PAGASA ENSO monitoring, updated 7 Oct 2026 · USDA FAS GAIN report RP2026-0032, 8 Oct 2026

5Primer No. 22: Fall armyworm

Fall armyworm: rotate by IRAC group, not by brand

First suspected in the Philippines at Piat, Cagayan, on 20 June 2019 and confirmed by DNA barcoding, fall armyworm has confirmed resistance elsewhere to IRAC Groups 1A, 1B, 3A, 11A and 15, and to Groups 28 and 5 in Puerto Rico. IRAC advises treatment windows of about 30 days with no more than two applications of the same mode of action, then rotating to a different group and never treating successive generations with the same group.

Why it mattersThe overseas resistance profile does not prove the same in Philippine fields, but a programme built on a single mode of action has no rotation partner.

Sources: IPPC pest report, 28 Oct 2019 · IRAC fall armyworm poster (Asia), undated

Primer note: FRAC Group 3 (DMI / triazole) fungicides

DMI fungicides inhibit sterol synthesis in fungi, and FRAC rates their resistance risk as medium. FRAC advises against repeated solo use: alternate or mix them with effective, non-cross-resistant partners.

Why it mattersRotation statements on DMI product labels and spray programmes should follow FRAC guidance.

Source: FRAC SBI working group, living page, undated
