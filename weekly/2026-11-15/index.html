1Global industry and trends

Corteva Q3: sales down 9%, Asia Pacific up on volume

Corteva's Q3 2026 results, released on 11 November, are its first as a single crop protection business, so the crop protection figure is the company total. Net sales were $1.59 billion, down 9%, with volume down 2% and price down 7%. Operating EBITDA, adjusted pro forma, fell 17% to $222 million from $268 million, and operating EPS fell 57% to $0.06. Asia Pacific net sales were $208 million, up 3% reported and 5% organic, with volume up 7% and price down 2%. Latin America fell 28% to $570 million, down 31% organic, with volume down 24% and price down 7%; Corteva does not give these splits for Brazil alone. CEO Luke Kissam said: "Brazil was materially weaker than anticipated." The FY2026 outlook was cut to adjusted pro forma operating EBITDA of $1.10 to $1.125 billion and operating EPS of $0.76 to $0.80.

Why it mattersRising volume against falling price in Asia Pacific points to competition on cost: easier market entry for generic products, but thinner margins.

Sources: Corteva Q3 2026 release, 11 Nov 2026 · SEC EDGAR copy

US EPA cancels about 340 pesticide registrations

A cancellation order in the Federal Register (Vol. 91, No. 218, document 2026-23117) took effect on 13 November 2026. Its tables list 125 voluntary cancellations (Table 1) and 215 for unpaid maintenance fees (Table 3), about 340 in all; EPA does not print a total. Products named include Syngenta's Touchdown Total (glyphosate), Karate with Zeon Technology (lambda-cyhalothrin) and Agri-Mek SC (abamectin), Lannate (methomyl) and ADAMA Bravo-branded chlorothalonil. Several entries are state-specific FIFRA 24(c) registrations, so this is not a nationwide cancellation of every registration carrying those brand names. Registrants may generally sell and distribute qualifying Table 1 stocks until 15 January 2027, and other persons may sell until stocks are exhausted.

Why it mattersA US cancellation does not itself cancel a Philippine registration, but it changes what a supplier can say about a product's current US status.

Sources: govinfo, FR doc 2026-23117, 13 Nov 2026 · Federal Register, 13 Nov 2026 · Global Agriculture, 14 Nov 2026

FPA posts MC 2026-17, MC 2026-16 and AO 2026-01

Three FPA issuances were published on 27 October 2026. MC 2026-17 requires a batch or lot number on all fertilizer product labels and packaging; it is signed by Executive Director Glenn D.C. Estrada and "shall take effect immediately". Administrative Order No. 2026-01 sets emergency regulatory measures for fertilizer, pesticide and other agricultural chemical trade and product registration during an emergency, armed conflict or severe supply disruption; its signatory and effectivity are not given here. All three PDFs are image-only scans, and no transition period or fee amount is quoted here. MC 2026-15, MC 2026-13 and MC 2026-04 do not appear on the FPA website as of 15 November.

Why it mattersMC 2026-17 means a label and packaging change for every registered fertilizer product, effective immediately on the face of the scan.

5Primer: Fusarium wilt of banana, Tropical Race 4

Reference material, not dated news. This primer draws on standard disease biology and the FRAC Code List.

What TR4 is and how to recognise it

Fusarium wilt of banana, historically Panama disease, is caused by the soil-borne fungus Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. cubense (Foc). Race 1 ended the dominance of Gros Michel in the Latin American export trade; Tropical Race 4 attacks Cavendish, the world's export banana today. TR4 was first reported in Southeast Asia, reached Colombia in 2019, and was confirmed in Peru by SENASA on 12 April 2021. The fungus enters through the roots and blocks water transport in the xylem. Older leaves yellow first, then petioles collapse so leaves hang like a skirt. The diagnostic sign is reddish-brown to dark vascular staining inside the pseudostem or corm. Confirming TR4 itself needs laboratory work.

Why it mattersInternal staining separates Fusarium wilt from bacterial wilt and drought stress, which can mimic the leaf symptoms.

Source: FAO World Banana Forum, TR4 in Peru, 23 Apr 2021

A quarantine and sanitation disease first

TR4 survives in soil as chlamydospores for decades without a host. There is no curative treatment and no fungicide that reliably protects a Cavendish plantation in infested soil. The fungus moves in soil, water, machinery, footwear and, above all, infected planting material. Management rests on tissue-culture planting material, footbaths and equipment disinfection, restricted vehicle movement, drainage, containing infected mats in place, and resistant or tolerant cultivars. Trichoderma and Bacillus agents, silicon and soil amendments suppress rather than eliminate, and the evidence for them is variable.

Why it mattersChemistry comes a distant third, after quarantine and plant breeding.

Cyclobutrifluram, FRAC Group 7, and the Davao trial

Syngenta's Tymirium technology is built on cyclobutrifluram, a succinate dehydrogenase inhibitor positioned for nematodes and soil-borne Fusarium. FRAC places it in Group 7, the SDHI group, with boscalid, fluopyram, fluxapyroxad and penthiopyrad, and classifies the group as medium to high resistance risk. The Philippine registration status of cyclobutrifluram or any Tymirium product is not established here. A DA-Davao study launched in May 2026 in Braulio E. Dujali, Davao del Norte, compares conventional farming with Tymirium and with Tymirium plus Trichoderma across about 672 banana plantlets, with Yara Fertilizers Philippines Inc. and Syngenta Philippines as partners.

Why it mattersThe Davao study is a trial, not proof of efficacy or a registered TR4 claim.

Sources: FRAC Code List 2026 · SunStar, 18 May 2026
