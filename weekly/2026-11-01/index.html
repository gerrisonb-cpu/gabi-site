The Green Brief - 1 November 2026

Sunday, 1 November 2026 · Covers 25 October to 1 November 2026 · Every item checked against a dated public source

This week in Philippine & global agrochem

FPA posted Week 42 and Week 43 fertilizer prices, and DAP is up 8.5% in two weeks to ₱3,213.22 a bag. The peso hit a record low, SRA wants ₱2 billion with 40% of a proposed pest package for the red-striped soft scale insect, and El Niño is cutting the palay outlook. 1Global industry and trends

FMC posts a $2.07 billion Q3 loss as its pricing overhaul takes hold

Reported revenue was $106 million against $542 million a year earlier, down 80%. Underlying revenue excluding India and one-time actions was $745 million, down 22%, and the commercial actions cut revenue by about $639 million. The figures were disclosed in a Form 8-K filed on 29 October 2026.

Why it mattersFMC's diamide products are on Philippine labels. A distributor-inventory reset of this size can change supply plans and the timing of new registrations.

Sources: Global Agriculture, 30 Oct 2026 · Global Agriculture, 30 Oct 2026

Other Q3 results: ADAMA's Asia-Pacific grows, BASF Agricultural Solutions turns negative

  • ADAMA's Q3 revenue fell 4% to $897 million, but Asia-Pacific rose 4% to $235 million, one of only two regions in growth. Source: Global Agriculture, 30 Oct 2026
  • BASF Agricultural Solutions' Q3 sales fell 19.7% to €1,404 million and EBITDA before special items was minus €48 million. BASF says Asia-Pacific sales were down considerably, with herbicide volumes hit by El Niño weather. Its Agricultural Solutions IPO remains on track for mid-2027. Source: BASF Quarterly Statement Q3 2026, 28 Oct 2026 · AgNavigator, 28 Oct 2026
  • Syngenta Group's nine-month sales were $17.3 billion, down 5%, with EBITDA up 1% to $3.2 billion. Q3 sales fell 10% to $5.2 billion. Source: Syngenta Group, Oct 2026
  • DCM Shriram's Bioseed revenue fell to ₹73 crore from ₹86 crore, which the company put down to El Niño-related weather and demand in India and the Philippines. Source: Global Agriculture, 30 Oct 2026

Why it mattersAsia-Pacific held up for ADAMA while El Niño hit BASF's herbicide volumes in the same region, so the weather is now showing up in company results.

EU sets a prothioconazole limit on rice of 0.03 mg/kg, in force about 5 November

Commission Regulation (EU) 2026/2289, published 16 October 2026, revises maximum residue limits for acequinocyl, deltamethrin, dodine, maleic hydrazide, pinoxaden and prothioconazole. It enters into force twenty days after publication, about 5 November 2026. The limits apply to all food sold in the EU, domestic or imported.

Why it mattersThis is an import standard, so it reaches any Philippine food consignment to the EU whatever the local label allows. A limit this low makes pre-harvest interval and spray timing an export question.

Source: Global Agriculture, 26 Oct 2026

Big-five R&D is shifting toward biologicals

An AgbioInvestor survey for CropLife International, covering BASF, Bayer, Corteva, FMC and Syngenta, found biologicals R&D averaged $37 million per company in 2023, up 43.4% on 2019, while chemical R&D fell 9.3% to $336 million. Conventional chemistry is still about 90% of the combined budget. Bringing one new chemical active to US and European markets costs about $307 million over about 11.4 years.

Why it mattersMore biostimulants, microbials and biocontrol agents are heading to market. FPA regulates these under different categories and data requirements from conventional chemistry.

Source: Global Agriculture, 30 Oct 2026

UK pesticide register adds new approvals, with no withdrawals

The October update from HSE's Chemicals Regulation Division, published 5 October 2026, includes Syngenta Miravis Era (prothioconazole plus pydiflumetofen), BASF Revysense (mefentrifluconazole), FMC Benevia 10 OD and Preza 10 OD (both cyantraniliprole), and two Sharda deltamethrin grain-store products.

Why it mattersApprovals at a major regulator are a free early signal of the actives and mixtures that may reach FPA in the next few years.

Source: Global Agriculture, 26 Oct 2026

Also in the news

  • US EPA registered BASF's Ridivex, a three-active corn herbicide premix (diflufenzopyr, dicamba and pyroxasulfone), on 19 October 2026. US corn only. Source: Global Agriculture, 30 Oct 2026
  • Australia's APVMA completed 43.9% of major agricultural chemical applications on time in January to March 2026, by its own quarterly report. Source: Global Agriculture, 26 Oct 2026
  • USDA picked eight US fertilizer projects for about $573 million in proposed funding. Its modelling projects nitrogen prices down 4.4% and potash down 5.3%, tied to capacity not yet built. Source: USDA, 22 Oct 2026
  • Codex's contaminants committee (CCCF19) is working on a cadmium code covering rice, seaweed and cocoa beans, expected to be finalised in 2027. Source: Global Agriculture, 28 Oct 2026

Why it mattersBackground on regulator workloads, future fertilizer supply and food-safety standards that touch rice.

2Company and innovation news

None of these announcements names the Philippines as a market. They are included as signals of where crop-protection technology is heading.

PI Industries launches pioxaniliprole, India's first home-grown insecticide active

PI unveiled pioxaniliprole on 29 October 2026. Its 31 October launch release gives the brand as Pioxazon, a 20% SC, for yellow stem borer in rice, fall armyworm in maize and pod borer in Bengal gram. Indian press reports also list rice leaf folder, and describe it as a diamide acting on the ryanodine receptor.

Why it mattersA new insecticide active with rice stem borer on its label. Its mode-of-action group should be confirmed against the official IRAC listing before it is treated as Group 28.

Sources: PI Industries, 29 Oct 2026 · PI Industries, 31 Oct 2026 · Indian Express, 29 Oct 2026

SOLASTA Bio files its first peptide insecticide in the US

The UK company's active ingredient and first end-use product are under review by US EPA and California's DPR. EPA classified the lead peptide as biochemical, a faster pathway. The first target is aphids in leafy greens and vegetables, and the company is seeking IRAC recognition as a new mode of action.

Why it mattersA sign of where biological insect control is heading. How a regulator classifies a product like this decides the data it must supply.

Source: AgriBusiness Global, 28 Oct 2026

Global biocontrol market estimated at $9.83 billion in 2026

DunhamTrimmer, presenting at ABIM 2026 in Basel, projects $14.97 billion by 2032, a 7.3% annual growth rate, with Asia-Pacific reaching $1.76 billion. Growth is slowing in mature markets.

Why it mattersMarket estimates help explain why more biological products are being developed, but they are projections, not sales.

Source: Global Agriculture, 26 Oct 2026

Peso hits a record low, and DA says imported fertilizer and rice will cost more

The peso reached about ₱64.17 to the dollar on 27 October. DA has deferred or rescheduled its own fertilizer purchases because a bag once budgeted at ₱1,500 now costs ₱2,800 to ₱2,900. Imported rice on 28 October was ₱56.09 a kg for premium and ₱48.58 for well-milled.

Why it mattersA weaker peso lifts the landed cost of imported fertilizer, as the Philippines imports nearly all of its nitrogen and phosphate.

Sources: BusinessWorld, 29 Oct 2026 · Philippine Star, 1 Nov 2026

Chinese technical prices turn higher as regulators enforce cost-based pricing

China's crop-protection association and its price regulators have moved against below-cost selling, and draft safety rules for nitro-chemistry constrain supply. The Sino-Agri Leading technical price index stood at 75.25 on 18 October 2026, up 2.53% (sources differ on whether that is week on week or month on month).

International fertilizer: nitrogen still the sharpest mover

DTN's US retail survey for the second week of October put urea at $674 per short ton, up 13% year on year, and potash at $503, up 3%. The Hormuz conflict is in its ninth month and Nutrien has shut its Trinidad nitrogen operations indefinitely. The World Bank's fertilizer index fell 22% quarter on quarter in Q3 2026 but is still up 14% for 2026 as a whole.

Why it mattersNitrogen is the sharpest mover and potash the mildest, which fits FPA's own prints: prilled urea and DAP firming, MOP easing.

Sources: Global Agriculture, 26 Oct 2026 · World Bank, 30 Oct 2026

4Emerging crops and pests

SRA seeks ₱2 billion, with 40% of a pest package for the red-striped soft scale insect

SRA wants ₱2 billion under the Sugarcane Industry Development Act. Of a proposed ₱1 billion for irrigation and pest management, 60% would go to irrigation and 40% to RSSI control: drone spraying equipment, fungal biocontrol and regional laboratories. No active ingredient or product is named.

Why it mattersThe plan leans toward biocontrol and application equipment rather than conventional chemistry. Any drone recommendation should be checked against FPA's list of products approved for drone use, last updated as of 30 September 2026.

Sources: Manila Times, 26 Oct 2026 · FPA drone-use list, as of 30 Sep 2026

El Niño cuts the palay outlook

DA projects palay falling from a 20.7 million tonne target toward about 19.7 million tonnes, and PAGASA expects the driest months from November to April, covering the dry-season rice crop. PSA puts Q3 palay output down 17.8%. The Office of Civil Defense counts 109 areas under a state of calamity this year, with ₱6.8 billion in agricultural damage.

Why it mattersDrought is not a pest problem, but it raises demand for soil conditioners, water-retention products and foliar nutrition, and land left unplanted buys no inputs.

Sources: Global Agriculture, 26 Oct 2026 · Manila Times, 31 Oct 2026 · Global Agriculture, 30 Oct 2026

SOCCSKSARGEN farmers get 50% more RCEF rice seed for the 2027 dry season

The allocation rises from 40 kg to 60 kg of certified inbred seed per beneficiary, about 286,284 bags for nearly 100,000 smallholder rice farmers. More than 80% of the region's farmers direct-seed.

Why it mattersDirect-seeded rice carries heavier weed and rodent pressure than transplanted rice, so this reads as a herbicide and rodenticide demand signal for Region 12.

Source: DA-PhilRice, 30 Oct 2026

5Primer: Rice leaf folder

Rice leaf folder: often sprayed when it does not need to be

The larva of Cnaphalocrocis medinalis folds a rice leaf lengthwise, stitches it with silk and feeds inside, leaving white streaks. Deadheart and whitehead are stem borer signs, not leaf folder. Modern rice makes up for early leaf loss, and research in Vietnam found spraying for leaf folder in the first 40 days after sowing was not needed. Philippine research found an action threshold of 10 to 15% leaf damage gave the right spray decision 93 to 99% of the time.

Why it mattersThresholds and crop stage, not calendar spraying, should drive the decision.

Sources: PhilRice, Harmful Organisms in Philippine Rice Fields, Feb 2023 · Heong and others, Crop Protection 17(5), 1998 · Litsinger and others, Int. J. Pest Management 52, 2006

Diamide resistance is now documented, so rotate by IRAC group

Chlorantraniliprole resistance in leaf folder reached 64.9 to 113.7 times in Chinese field populations in 2022, with cross-resistance across the diamides, and India has reported field resistance to flubendiamide and lambda-cyhalothrin. Rotating one diamide with another is not resistance management, because they share Group 28. Other groups used against leaf folder include 5 (spinosyns), 6 (avermectins), 11A (Bt) and 14 (cartap, which is not a carbamate or organophosphate).

Why it mattersThe overseas results do not prove the same in Philippine fields, but a programme built on one mode of action has no rotation partner.

Sources: Sun and others, Pest Management Science 79(9), 2023 · IRAC Mode of Action Classification 11.5, Feb 2026
