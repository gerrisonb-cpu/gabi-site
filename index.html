The FPA path for a new pesticide

  1. 01Experimental Use PermitEUP application to the FPA.
  2. 02Field trialsBioefficacy and, where needed, residue trials by an FPA-accredited researcher.
  3. 03Registration dossierDocuments, specifications and confirmatory analysis of a product sample.
  4. 04Certificate of Product RegistrationThen renewals, label changes and upkeep.

Simplified. Requirements differ by product; we confirm yours at the start.

What we do

Regulatory work for farm inputs, end to end

Our core work is with the Fertilizer and Pesticide Authority. We take a product from the first gap check through filing and FPA review, then keep registrations current.

01

Product registration

Pesticide and fertilizer registrations with the FPA, for new and generic products.

02

Renewals and amendments

Renewals, amendments and replies to FPA deficiency letters, with deadlines tracked.

03

Labels and FPA comments

Label drafting and revisions that answer FPA comments.

04

EUPs and field trials

Experimental Use Permits and coordination of trials with FPA-accredited researchers.

05

Support for foreign principals

Registrations held for foreign principals, with letters of authorization in place.

06

FDA registration

For products that fall under the Philippine FDA rather than the FPA.

07

Regulatory intelligence

FPA policy, prices and industry news, tracked every week.

08

Market entry and distributors

Market-entry assessment and matching with Philippine distributors.

What you can expect when you work with us.

  • FPA work is our core.

    GABI is an agricultural consultancy built around registrations with the Fertilizer and Pesticide Authority.

  • Local and foreign manufacturers.

    We work with makers in the Philippines and abroad, and, where agreed, can act as local registrant for a foreign principal.

  • The whole lifecycle, one point of contact.

    Gap check, EUP, trials, registration, label changes and renewals, with one point of contact at GABI.

  • Clear about what the FPA decides.

    We prepare, file and follow up. Approvals and timelines are the FPA's decision, and our proposals say so in writing.

  • We read the rules every week.

    The Green Brief tracks FPA prices, policy and pest news, each item checked against a dated source.

  • Farmer-centric.

    We keep Filipino farmers in view in the work we do.
